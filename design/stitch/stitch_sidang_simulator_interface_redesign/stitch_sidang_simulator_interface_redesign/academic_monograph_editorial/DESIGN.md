## Brand & Style

This design system is engineered for the rigors of high-stakes academic discourse, specifically an Indonesian undergraduate and graduate thesis defense simulator (*Sidang Simulator*). The interface replicates the gravity, stillness, and authority of a defense chamber, paired with the tactile precision of a physical scholarly monograph and legal transcript.

The emotional tone is deliberate, intellectually focused, and sobering. It avoids playful gamification, flashy software idioms, or distracting SaaS novelties. Instead, it frames user inquiries, examiner rebuttals, thesis excerpts, and academic evaluations as tangible manuscripts printed on archive-grade paper. 

### Design Movement: Classical Editorial & Book Arts
- **Paper Realism over Skeuomorphism:** Achieved entirely through tonal paper layering, authentic hairline typographic rules, and ink-bleed contrasting values rather than skeuomorphic textures or drop shadows.
- **Typographic Primacy:** Headings, thesis excerpts, and examiner interrogatives hold structural dominance through literary serifs, while utilitarian telemetry and inputs are anchored by razor-sharp neo-grotesque sans-serifs.
- **Academic Seriousness:** Layouts favor linear or asymmetric split-register columns reminiscent of critical editions, legal briefs, and annotated peer-reviewed journals.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration from archival printing: warm unbleached rag paper, iron-gall and carbon ink, bookbinder cloth, and fountain pen inks. 

### Surface Roles
- **Canvas Base (`#FBFBFA` / `#F5F5F0`):** The unbleached warm pulp foundation of the entire workspace.
- **Document / Manuscript Tile (`#FFFFFF`):** High-grade crisp paper stock reserved for active defense transcripts, thesis draft presentations, and primary reading surfaces.
- **Recessed Surface (`#F4F3ED`):** Muted warm cream used for sidebar dossiers, examiner note boards, and meta panes.

### Ink & Typography Roles
- **Primary Ink (`#181A19`):** Carbon-black with an imperceptible touch of organic warmth, eliminating the clinical coldness of pure `#000000` while preserving stark editorial contrast.
- **Secondary Ink / Meta (`#59615C`):** Editorial slate for metadata, rubric criteria, paragraph references, and passive panel headers.
- **Muted Rules (`#E4E3DC` / `#D8D6CD`):** Structural hairlines mimicking bookbinder margins and ruled paper sheets.

### Accent & Functional Roles
- **Oxford Navy (`#1B2D45`):** The primary academic authority color. Used for focal CTAs, current-speaker callouts, and key defense milestones.
- **Forest Ink (`#2D5A46`):** Scholarly validation, rubric approvals, and positive advisor notations.
- **Sepia Ink (`#8C5E19` over `#FEF7EE`):** Academic critique, defense examiner objections, revisions required, and timed alert intervals.

## Typography

Typography governs the authority of the workspace. We pair **Newsreader** (a purpose-built optical serif for sustained academic immersion and editorial prose) with **Inter** (for micro-metrics, telemetry, and structured rubric forms).

### Editorial Rules
- **Examiner Questions & Thesis Excerpts:** Must always render in `Newsreader` (`headline-md` or `body-lg`). The deliberate rhythm of serif type elevates the critical weight of questions raised by the defense committee.
- **Labels & Sub-headers:** Must be rendered in `Inter` with small uppercase tracking (`label-md` or `label-sm`, letter spacing `0.04em`–`0.06em`) to echo academic catalog indexing (e.g., `BAB II: TINJAUAN PUSTAKA`, `PENGUJI I: METODOLOGI`).
- **Line Length and Leading:** For transcript reading panes, enforce a strict optimal line measure of 60 to 75 characters (`max-w-prose`) with relaxed leading (`1.875rem` for 18px text) to honor physical manuscript typography.

## Layout & Spacing

The workspace uses a structured asymmetric grid inspired by university dissertations and legal case dossiers. Rather than floating software cards, the layout relies on continuous paper columns partitioned by structural 1px hairlines (`#E4E3DC`).

### Grid Structure
- **Desktop (1024px+):** A 12-column layout. Typically partitioned as a 3-column dossier/index on the left (candidate credentials, thesis metadata, rubric checkmarks), a 6-column active defense transcript center stage (the monograph sheet), and a 3-column examiner desk on the right (real-time cross-examinations, feedback, defense clock).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** A split-register 2-column model. Left pane holds the active transcript; right pane switches via tabs between rubric and examiner inquiries.
- **Mobile (<768px):** Single-column stacked manuscript view. Secondary modules dock into bottom sheets anchored by clean dividing borders.

### Spacing Principles
- Use vertical rhythm driven by `space-md` (16px) and `space-xl` (40px) to separate distinct scholarly discourse units (e.g., between an examiner's interrogation and the student's defense statement).
- Inner card/module padding must remain consistent at `1.25rem` to `1.5rem` (`space-lg`), avoiding tight claustrophobic SaaS metrics.

## Elevation & Depth

This system outright rejects drop shadows, diffuse ambient blurs, and glassmorphic overlays. Depth is achieved strictly through **Tonal Paper Layering** and **Low-Contrast Hairline Outlines**.

### Structural Principles
- **Plane 0 (Canvas Base):** Warm paper `#F5F5F0`. Represents the defense room work table.
- **Plane 1 (Manuscript & Modules):** Pure sheet `#FFFFFF` bordered with a 1px solid rule (`#E4E3DC`).
- **Plane 2 (Notations, Footnotes, Marginalia):** Pale warm cream `#F4F3ED` or warning parchment `#FEF7EE` nested seamlessly within Plane 1, bounded by matching hairline dividers.
- **Overlays & Dialogs:** When modal states are mandatory (e.g., *Yudisium* / Final Verdict Declaration), use a solid `#181A19` backdrop with 40% opacity, revealing a central crisp white paper sheet framed by a distinct 2px double border (`#1B2D45` outer, `#E4E3DC` inner) rather than a blurred shadow.

## Shapes

The geometric signature of this design system is **Sharp (`0`)**. 

To preserve the tactile reality of physical paper, bound folios, index cards, and printed certificates, all containers, input fields, badges, and buttons default to `0px` border-radius (`rounded-none`). Where touch targets require slight tactile softening on mobile, a maximum radius of `2px` (`rounded-xs`) is allowed exclusively for small chip indicators. Pill badges and rounded organic shapes are strictly prohibited.

## Components

### Buttons & Action Triggers
- **Primary Action (e.g., "Ajukan Argumen", "Mulai Sesi Tanya Jawab"):** Solid Oxford Navy (`#1B2D45`), white sharp text (`Inter`, `label-lg`), 0px radius, 1px solid `#1B2D45` border. On hover: `#122033`. Focus state: 1px offset outline of `#1B2D45`.
- **Secondary Action (e.g., "Buka Catatan Revisi"):** Unfilled background, 1px solid `#D8D6CD` border, text `#181A19`. On hover: `#F4F3ED`.
- **Destructive/Concede Action (e.g., "Tarik Pernyataan"):** Unfilled background, 1px solid `#8C5E19` border, text `#8C5E19`.

### Academic Chips & Status Indicators
- Never use soft, rounded pill badges.
- Render as square-cornered, rectangular metadata tags: `px-2 py-0.5`, uppercase `label-sm`, 1px solid border.
- **Status - Sedang Diuji (In Progress):** Border `#1B2D45`, text `#1B2D45`, background `#FFFFFF`.
- **Status - Perlu Revisi (Revisions Needed):** Border `#8C5E19`, text `#8C5E19`, background `#FEF7EE`.
- **Status - Diterima (Accepted):** Border `#2D5A46`, text `#2D5A46`, background `#F5FAF7`.

### Input Fields & Response Terminal
- Text input areas must feel like an open writer’s desk or typewritten statement sheet.
- Border: 1px solid `#D8D6CD` at rest; 1.5px solid `#1B2D45` on focus. No glowing aura or box-shadow ring.
- Background: `#FFFFFF`. Typography: `Inter` `body-md` for user responses, accompanied by an optional toggle to view drafting in `Newsreader`.

### Lists & Rubric Trees
- Separate list items exclusively with 1px horizontal rules (`#E4E3DC`), eliminating standalone floating card containers.
- Rubric scoring criteria employ small classical square indicators (`12px x 12px`, sharp corners) with filled ink checks rather than consumer-style round radio circles.

### Examiner Question Cards (Dialogue Blocks)
- Instead of high-elevation cards, dialogue blocks use an editorial pull-quote style: a left accent border (2.5px solid Oxford Navy `#1B2D45` or Sepia `#8C5E19`), set against `#FBFBFA` with `1.25rem` padding.
- Examiner name and academic credentials render in small tracked sans-serif (`label-sm`), while the formal thesis question is typeset in `Newsreader` italic `headline-sm`.

### Monograph Footnotes & Marginalia
- Annotations appear docked to the right edge or in a dedicated gutter. Numbered with superscript traditional numerals (`¹`, `²`), rendered in `#59615C` at `body-sm`.
